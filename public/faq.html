Getting Started

We accept a wide range of file formats:
  • Vector files (recommended): PDF, AI, EPS, SVG
  • Raster files: PNG, JPG, JPEG, TIFF
For best results, we recommend uploading vector files (PDF or AI) as they maintain quality at any size and preserve your exact CMYK colors.
You can explore the uploader and position your artwork without logging in. However, you'll need to log in to your account when you're ready to select a product and add items to your cart. This ensures your orders are properly linked to your account.
We offer several transfer types to suit different needs:
  • Full Colour: For multi-color designs with photographic quality
  • HD Transfers: High-definition prints for detailed artwork
  • Single Colour: Cost-effective option for one-color designs
  • Metallic: Gold and silver metallic finishes
  • Zero: No-feel transfers for a soft hand
  • DTF: Direct-to-film transfers
  • Reflective: Hi-visibility reflective transfers

Artwork & Design

CMYK (Cyan, Magenta, Yellow, Black) is the color model used in printing. Unlike RGB colors used on screens, CMYK colors are specifically designed for print production. Our system preserves your CMYK colors throughout the entire process, ensuring your printed transfers match your original design exactly.
The red dotted lines represent a 3mm safety margin. Keep your artwork within these boundaries to ensure nothing gets cut off during production. The alignment tools will automatically align your artwork to these safe boundaries.

Technical Questions

Blurry logos usually indicate a low-resolution raster image. For best results, upload vector files (PDF, AI, SVG) which scale perfectly at any size. If you only have a raster image, ensure it's at least 300 DPI at the final print size. The pre-flight check will warn you if your image resolution is too low.
Some artwork files contain very complex paths or effects that may cause processing issues. If you see this message, you can use our Dropbox File Request option to upload the file. We'll process it manually and notify you when it's ready. Alternatively, you can simplify your artwork in your design software before re-uploading.
Computer monitors display colors using RGB (light), while printing uses CMYK (ink). Some bright colors visible on screen cannot be exactly reproduced in print. We recommend working in CMYK mode in your design software and using our CMYK color preservation feature to ensure the most accurate color reproduction possible.
